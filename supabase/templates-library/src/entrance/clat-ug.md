# English Language

## Skills Tested

- Main point and arguments of a passage
- Inferences and conclusions
- Summarising a passage
- Comparing arguments and viewpoints
- Meaning of words and phrases in context

## Question Types

- Reading comprehension
- Vocabulary in context
- Author's tone and intent

# Current Affairs, including General Knowledge

## Areas

- Contemporary events of significance from India and the world
- Arts and culture
- International affairs
- Historical events of continuing significance

## Frequent Themes

- Legal and policy news
- Constitutional and governance developments
- Sports

# Legal Reasoning

## Skills Tested

- Identifying rules and principles in a passage
- Applying rules to fact situations
- How changes to a rule change its application

## Frequent Areas of Law

- Law of contract
- Law of torts
- Criminal law
- Constitutional law
- Environmental law
- Family law
- Intellectual property law

# Logical Reasoning

## Skills Tested

- Arguments: premises and conclusions
- Patterns of reasoning
- Strengthening and weakening arguments
- Inferences and applying them
- Analogies, contradictions and equivalence

## Question Types

- Assumptions
- Inferences
- Strengthen and weaken
- Conclusions

# Quantitative Techniques

## Areas (Class 10 level)

- Ratios and proportions
- Basic algebra
- Mensuration
- Statistical estimation

## Frequent Question Types

- Percentages
- Data interpretation: tables and graphs
- Arithmetic word problems
