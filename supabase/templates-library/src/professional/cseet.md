- Words with multiple meanings
- One word substitution
- Words frequently mis-spelt
- Homophones
- Idioms and phrases
- Proverbs
- Abbreviations
- Para jumbles
- Sentence completion, arrangement and correction
- Foreign words and phrases commonly used

- Business communication through social media

- High technology in business communication: AI, chatbots, blogs, social media

- Intranet: benefit and purpose

- Advantages and disadvantages of email
- Notice, notes on agenda, minutes and action taken report

- Terms defined under laws, rules and regulations, including financial and non-financial terms
