- Exercise for articulation

- Exercise for articulation

- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

# Grades 6 to 8

## Grade 6

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: C major, Ab major, G# minor, E major, C minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on C, Dominant 7th in the key of Db, Diminished 7th starting on C
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 7

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: B major, Eb minor, A minor, Eb major, G minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on B, Diminished 7th starting on B, Whole-tone scale starting on Eb
- Also: Dominant 7th in the key of D
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 8

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: Bb major, Db major, F# major, F# minor, D major, F major, C# minor, G major, D minor
- Scales and arpeggios: C major, G minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on Bb, Whole-tone scale starting on C# slurred, Dominant 7th in the key of F#
- Also: Diminished 7th starting on F#, Diminished 7th starting on Bb, Dominant 7th in the key of Eb
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Exercise for articulation
- Exercise for finger technique
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)
