# Initial and Grades 1 to 3

## Initial

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: F major, G major
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 1

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: F major, E minor, G major
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Exercise for articulation
- Exercise for finger technique
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 2

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: D major, G minor, Bb major, A minor
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 3

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: G major, A major, F major, G minor, A minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on G
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

# Grades 4 and 5

## Grade 4

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: G major, Eb major, B minor, C major, D minor, E minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on D, Dominant 7th in the key of G, Dominant 7th in the key of C
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 5

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: E major, Eb major, F minor, F# minor, Ab major, A major, C# minor, C minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on E, Dominant 7th in the key of Ab, Diminished 7th starting on F#
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

# Grades 6 to 8

## Grade 6

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: Bb major, F# minor, Bb minor, F# major
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on Bb, Dominant 7th in the key of B, Diminished 7th starting on Bb
- Also: Whole-tone scale starting on D
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 7

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: B minor, Eb minor, A major, B major, G minor, A minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on B, Whole-tone scale starting on A, Dominant 7th in the key of D
- Also: Dominant 7th in the key of Ab, Diminished 7th starting on B
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)

## Grade 8

- Three pieces
- Scales and arpeggios: C major, E major, F minor, Ab major, G# minor, C# minor, D minor, F major, A minor
- Scales and arpeggios: G major, E minor, G minor
- Also: Chromatic scale starting on C, Whole-tone scale starting on C#, Dominant 7th in the key of F#
- Also: Diminished 7th starting on Ab, Diminished 7th starting on C
- Exercise for tone and phrasing
- Exercise for articulation
- Exercise for finger technique
- Supporting tests: two from sight reading, aural, improvisation and musical knowledge (options vary by grade)
