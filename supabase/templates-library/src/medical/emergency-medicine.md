# Resuscitation, Airway and Trauma

## Cardiovascular emergencies

- Basic and advanced life support
- Neonatal, paediatric and pregnancy CPR
- Acute coronary syndromes and thrombolysis
- Pulmonary embolism and aortic dissection
- Heart failure and pulmonary oedema
- Tachycardias, bradycardias and cardioversion
- Hypertensive emergencies
- Shock: haemorrhagic, cardiogenic, septic, anaphylactic
- Pericarditis, tamponade and endocarditis
- Vascular access and haemodynamic monitoring

## Airway management and anaesthesia

- Difficult airway management
- Rapid sequence intubation
- Non-invasive and mechanical ventilation
- Pain management and procedural sedation
- Local, regional and general anaesthesia
- Surgical airway

## Trauma

- Primary and secondary survey, ATLS
- Wound management and suturing
- Head, facial and dental injuries
- Spinal trauma and immobilisation
- Chest trauma
- Abdominal trauma, E-FAST and DPL
- Pelvic fractures
- Extremity trauma and compartment syndrome
- Trauma in children, pregnancy and the elderly
- Blast injuries and mass casualties

# Medical and Surgical Emergencies

## Respiratory emergencies

- Dyspnoea and respiratory failure
- ARDS
- Haemoptysis
- Acute severe asthma and COPD
- Pneumothorax and foreign body
- Pneumonia, empyema and lung abscess

## Gastrointestinal and hepatic emergencies

- Acute abdomen
- Pancreatitis, cholecystitis and appendicitis
- Intestinal obstruction and mesenteric ischaemia
- Dehydration and fluid therapy
- Upper and lower GI bleeding
- Acute liver failure and cirrhosis
- Jaundice and liver abscess

## Neurological emergencies

- Headache
- Syncope, vertigo and dizziness
- Seizures and status epilepticus
- Coma: metabolic and neurological
- Meningitis and encephalitis
- Acute stroke and TIA
- Myelopathies, GB syndrome and myasthenic crisis

## Genitourinary emergencies

- Renal colic and nephrolithiasis
- Acute kidney injury
- Acute urinary retention
- Testicular torsion
- Pyelonephritis
- Priapism and paraphimosis

## Eye, ENT and dental emergencies

- Red eye and acute glaucoma
- Ocular trauma and chemical burns
- Acute visual loss
- Stridor and upper airway obstruction
- Epistaxis
- Ear, nose and throat infections
- Dental fractures and avulsion

## Orthopaedic and rheumatological emergencies

- Acute osteomyelitis and septic arthritis
- Acute gout
- Back, neck and shoulder pain
- Hand and foot infections
- Acute vasculitis and antiphospholipid syndrome

## Haematology and oncology

- Bleeding disorders and DIC
- Anticoagulant and antiplatelet therapy
- Febrile neutropenia
- Anaemia and acute haemolysis
- Tumour lysis syndrome and cord compression
- Blood transfusion

## Infections

- Fever in the emergency department
- Malaria, dengue and leptospirosis
- Enteric fever
- Tetanus and rabies
- HIV and needlestick injury
- Sepsis
- Soft tissue infections

## Metabolic and endocrine emergencies

- Diabetic emergencies
- Fluid and electrolyte disorders
- Acid-base disturbances
- Adrenal and pituitary crises
- Thyroid storm and myxoedema coma

## Dermatological emergencies

- Exfoliative dermatitis
- SJS and TEN
- Skin infections and allergies

# Special Areas

## Toxicology

- Initial management and toxidromes
- Antidotes
- Insecticide and pesticide poisoning
- Drug overdoses
- Snake bites and stings
- Alcohol poisoning and withdrawal
- Caustics, metals and industrial toxins
- CBRN disasters

## Obstetric and gynaecological emergencies

- Ectopic pregnancy and abortion
- Pre-eclampsia and eclampsia
- Emergency delivery
- Antepartum and postpartum haemorrhage
- Sexual assault

## Paediatric emergencies

- Paediatric and neonatal life support
- Stridor, croup and asthma
- Fever and sepsis in children
- Dehydration
- Paediatric trauma and head injury
- Child abuse

## Environmental emergencies

- Burns and smoke inhalation
- Electrical and lightning injuries
- Heat and cold illnesses
- High altitude and diving illnesses
- Drowning
- Bites and envenomation

## Geriatric and psychiatric emergencies

- Geriatric assessment and abuse
- Self-harm and suicide
- Delirium and psychosis
- Substance abuse
- Violence in the emergency department

## Disaster medicine and miscellaneous

- Disaster planning
- Response to terrorist incidents
- Pre-hospital care
- Forensic and legal issues
- Palliative care and death notification
- Emergency imaging
