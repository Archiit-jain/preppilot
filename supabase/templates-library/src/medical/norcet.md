# Nursing Foundations

## Anatomy and Physiology

- Body systems: structure and function
- Cardiovascular and endocrine physiology
- Homeostasis

## Microbiology and Pathology

- Infection process and hospital-acquired infections
- Immunity
- Laboratory investigations

## Fundamentals of Nursing

- Nursing process
- Vital signs
- Infection control and biomedical waste management
- Patient safety and positioning
- Documentation
- Drug and IV fluid calculations

## Pharmacology

- Emergency drugs
- Antibiotics
- Insulin types
- Anticoagulants and antiplatelets
- Cardiac and CNS drugs
- Dosage calculations

# Clinical Nursing

## Medical-Surgical Nursing

- Cardiovascular conditions: MI, heart failure, arrhythmias, hypertension, shock
- Respiratory conditions: COPD, asthma, ARDS, oxygen therapy
- Neurological conditions: stroke, raised ICP, seizures
- Renal conditions: AKI, CKD and dialysis
- Endocrine conditions: diabetes, DKA, thyroid disorders
- Emergency and critical care

## Obstetric and Gynaecological Nursing

- Antenatal and postnatal care
- Labour management
- Fetal monitoring
- High-risk pregnancy and obstetric emergencies
- Newborn care
- Family planning

## Child Health Nursing

- Growth and developmental milestones
- Immunisation schedule
- Neonatal care
- IMNCI
- Paediatric emergencies and dosage

## Mental Health Nursing

- Psychiatric disorders
- Therapeutic communication
- Defence mechanisms
- Psychiatric therapies

# Community and Management

## Community Health Nursing

- National health programmes
- Epidemiology
- Communicable disease control
- Maternal and child health
- Levels of health care

## Nursing Management

- Leadership styles
- Quality assurance
- Ethical principles and legal responsibilities
- Documentation standards

# General Knowledge and Aptitude

## General Knowledge

- Current affairs
- Science and technology
- National events and health schemes

## Aptitude and Reasoning

- Percentages, ratio and proportion
- Time and work
- Profit and loss
- Series, coding-decoding and logical reasoning
