# Otorhinolaryngology and Ophthalmology

## ENT: Ear

- Anatomy and physiology of the ear
- Otitis externa and otitis media

- Vertigo and facial nerve palsy

- Epistaxis and nasal obstruction
- Rhinitis, polyps and sinusitis
- Nasal and sinus tumours

- Diseases of the tonsils and pharynx
- Laryngeal disorders and stridor
- Tracheostomy and airway emergencies

- Conjunctival and corneal disorders
- Uveitis

- Glaucoma

- Retinal disorders
- Optic nerve and visual pathway
- Errors of refraction and squint
- Ocular trauma and community ophthalmology
