# Paper I: Basic Sciences

## Applied anatomy and physiology

- Embryogenesis of organ systems
- Central and peripheral nervous systems
- Arterial and venous systems
- Surface anatomy of the spine, limbs, pelvis, chest and abdomen
- Growth, temperature regulation and fluid balance
- Acid-base and electrolyte balance
- Haematopoiesis and haemostasis
- Bone mineralisation: calcium-phosphate balance
- Renal, hepatic, respiratory and cardiac function
- Nutrition in orthopaedic and trauma patients

## Microbiology and pharmacology

- Microbiology of orthopaedic infections
- Sterilisation, disinfection and waste disposal
- Antimicrobials
- Analgesia and sedation
- Drug interactions and adverse effects
- Disease-modifying agents, chemotherapy and biologicals

## Healing

- Types and stages of wound healing
- Factors in wound healing
- Principles and biology of bone healing
- Factors and biomechanics of fracture healing

## Professionalism

- Professionalism and ethics
- Medico-legal essentials
- Research methodology and biostatistics

# Paper II: Traumatology and Rehabilitation

## Trauma care

- Mechanisms of injury and fracture biomechanics
- Shock and resuscitation
- Respiratory failure
- Haemostasis, coagulation and haemorrhage control
- Metabolic and endocrine response to trauma
- Early total care and damage control orthopaedics
- Triage of disasters and polytrauma

## Fractures and dislocations

- Classification of injuries in adults and children
- Principles of internal and external fixation
- Casts, splints and traction
- Closed reduction of common fractures and dislocations
- Nerve injuries

## Pain and rehabilitation

- Pain management and functional recovery
- Orthotics and prosthetics
- Rehabilitation teams
- Gait, posture and biomechanics

# Paper III: Orthopaedic Diseases

## Metabolic, congenital and developmental disorders

- Nutritional deficiency diseases of bone
- Depositional arthropathies
- Endocrine and metabolic bone disease
- Congenital anomalies
- Developmental skeletal disorders
- CTEV, DDH and Perthes disease

## Infections and arthritis

- Tuberculosis of bones and joints
- Pyogenic and fungal infections
- Musculoskeletal manifestations of HIV
- Rheumatoid arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis and seronegative arthritis
- Osteoarthritis and spondylosis
- Osteonecrosis

## Neuromuscular disorders

- Post-polio residual paralysis
- Cerebral palsy
- Muscular dystrophies and myopathies
- Entrapment neuropathies
- Spinal dysraphism and spinal anomalies

## Tumours

- Benign and malignant bone tumours
- Soft tissue tumours
- Metastatic bone disease

- Biomaterials and orthopaedic metallurgy
- Principles of joint replacement
