# Year 1

## Applied anatomy

- Diaphragm and the upper and lower airway
- Anatomy for regional, field and central neuraxial blocks
- Injections, arterial and venous cannulation and positioning

## Applied physics

- The anaesthesia machine
- Airway equipment: laryngoscopes and airway devices
- Breathing systems
- Minimum monitoring standards
- Gas laws and medical gas supply
- Fluidics
- Electricity and diathermy
- Oxygen therapy

## Applied physiology

- Theories of anaesthesia
- Respiratory, cardiovascular, hepatobiliary, renal and endocrine physiology
- Pregnancy, blood, muscle and the neuromuscular junction
- Nerve impulse transmission and ECG
- Temperature regulation, metabolism and the stress response
- Cerebral blood flow and intracranial pressure
- Central, autonomic and peripheral nervous systems

## Pharmacology

- Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics
- Drug interactions and anaphylactoid reactions
- Premedication and induction agents
- Intravenous and inhalational anaesthetics
- Neuromuscular blockers and reversal

## Fluids and basics of practice

- Fluid balance and blood transfusion
- Perioperative fluid therapy and acid-base balance
- General and regional anaesthetic techniques
- Pre-anaesthetic assessment and premedication
- Recovery and postoperative care
- Effects of positioning
- The operation theatre and post-anaesthesia care
- Introduction to acute and chronic pain
- Documentation, medico-legal aspects and informed consent

## Resuscitation and critical care

- Basic and advanced cardiac and trauma life support
- Neonatal resuscitation
- Introduction to mechanical ventilation
- Management of the unconscious patient
- Shock: pathophysiology and management
- Research methodology and biostatistics

# Year 2

## Equipment and monitoring

- Monitors, ventilators, vaporisers and fibreoptics
- Lasers, pacemakers and defibrillators
- Monitoring cardiac and respiratory function, blood gases and ICP
- Depth of anaesthesia and neuromuscular monitoring
- Sterilisation of equipment
- Blocks for chronic pain and chemical neurolysis

## Pharmacology and laboratory

- Drugs for cardiovascular, respiratory, endocrine, renal and CNS disease
- Blood gas interpretation and function tests
- Coagulation, its disturbances and blood components

## Special techniques and populations

- Outpatient and hypotensive anaesthesia
- Anaesthesia in rural areas, disasters and abnormal environments
- Associated medical disorders in surgical patients
- Geriatric and paediatric anaesthesia
- Emergency, ENT, orthopaedic and ophthalmic anaesthesia
- Obstetric, dental and radiology anaesthesia
- Postures and anaesthesia
- Induced hypothermia and patient safety

## Medical conditions

- Malignant hyperthermia
- Myasthenia gravis, GB syndrome and neuromuscular disease
- Obesity, COPD and bronchial asthma
- Diabetes mellitus and hypertensive crises
- Inherited metabolic diseases
- Care of the terminally ill and do-not-resuscitate orders

# Year 3

## Subspecialty anaesthesia

- Neuro, cardiac, thoracic and vascular anaesthesia
- Transplantation, burns and plastic surgery
- Severe cardiac, respiratory, renal or hepatic disease for unrelated surgery

## Critical care

- Shock and renal failure
- Ventilated and critically ill patients
- Multiple organ failure
- Infection control in OT and ICU
- Immune response, cytokines and anaesthesia
- Neonatal ventilation and critical care

## Pain

- Chronic pain therapy and therapeutic nerve blocks
- Acupuncture and non-conventional treatments

## Management and professional practice

- Equipment selection and maintenance
- Human resources and materials management
- Medical audit and critical incident reporting
- Ethics and clinical trials
- Hospital, ICU and OT design
- Medical education and evidence-based practice
