# Pathology

## General Pathology

- Cell injury, adaptation and cell death
- Acute and chronic inflammation
- Tissue repair and healing
- Haemodynamic disorders and shock
- Neoplasia
- Immunopathology

## Haematology

- Anaemia and its classification
- Leukaemia and lymphoma
- Bleeding and coagulation disorders
- Transfusion medicine

## Systemic Pathology

- Cardiovascular and respiratory pathology
- Gastrointestinal and hepatobiliary pathology
- Renal and urinary tract pathology
- Endocrine and reproductive pathology
- Central nervous system and musculoskeletal pathology

## Laboratory Medicine

- Clinical pathology and urine examination
- Cytopathology
- Autopsy pathology

# Microbiology

## General Microbiology

- Bacterial morphology and physiology
- Sterilisation and disinfection
- Culture media and identification
- Antimicrobial resistance

## Immunology

- Innate and adaptive immunity
- Antigen and antibody reactions
- Hypersensitivity reactions
- Autoimmunity and immunodeficiency

## Systematic Bacteriology

- Gram positive and gram negative organisms
- Mycobacteria
- Anaerobes and spirochaetes

## Virology, Mycology and Parasitology

- DNA and RNA viruses
- Hepatitis viruses and HIV
- Medical mycology
- Protozoa and helminths

## Applied Microbiology

- Hospital acquired infection and infection control
- Specimen collection and laboratory diagnosis
- Biomedical waste management

# Pharmacology

## General Pharmacology

- Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics
- Routes of drug administration
- Adverse drug reactions and pharmacovigilance
- Drug interactions
- Rational prescribing and essential medicines

## Autonomic Nervous System

- Cholinergic and anticholinergic drugs
- Adrenergic and antiadrenergic drugs
- Autacoids and related drugs

## Central Nervous System

- General and local anaesthetics
- Sedative-hypnotics and antiepileptics
- Antiparkinsonian drugs
- Antipsychotics, antidepressants and lithium
- Opioid and non-opioid analgesics

## Cardiovascular, Renal and Haematological Drugs

- Antihypertensive and antianginal drugs
- Drugs for heart failure and arrhythmias
- Diuretics
- Anticoagulants, antiplatelets and thrombolytics
- Hypolipidaemic drugs and haematinics

## Chemotherapy

- General principles of antimicrobial therapy
- Beta-lactams and other antibacterials
- Antitubercular and antileprotic drugs
- Antifungal, antiviral and antiretroviral drugs
- Antimalarial and anthelmintic drugs
- Anticancer and immunosuppressant drugs

## Endocrine, Gastrointestinal and Respiratory Drugs

- Corticosteroids and thyroid drugs
- Insulin and oral hypoglycaemics
- Drugs for peptic ulcer and emesis
- Drugs for asthma and COPD
- Toxicology and antidotes

# Forensic Medicine and Toxicology

## Forensic Pathology

- Death, its types and postmortem changes
- Identification
- Autopsy and exhumation

## Clinical Forensic Medicine

- Mechanical, thermal and regional injuries
- Asphyxial deaths
- Sexual offences
- Infant deaths and infanticide

## Medical Jurisprudence

- Legal procedures and court testimony
- Consent, negligence and professional misconduct
- Medical ethics and relevant acts

## Toxicology

- General principles and management of poisoning
- Corrosive, irritant and metallic poisons
- Neurotoxic, cardiotoxic and asphyxiant poisons
- Drug dependence and abuse

# Community Medicine

## Concepts of Health, Disease and Epidemiology

- Concept of health and disease
- Natural history of disease and levels of prevention
- Principles and methods of epidemiology
- Epidemiological study designs
- Screening and surveillance

## Biostatistics and Demography

- Data collection and presentation
- Measures of central tendency and dispersion
- Tests of significance and sampling
- Demography and family planning
- Population dynamics

## Communicable Diseases

- Epidemiology and control of respiratory infections
- Intestinal and arthropod-borne infections
- Zoonoses and surface infections
- Immunisation and the cold chain

## Non-Communicable Diseases and Health Programmes

- Epidemiology of non-communicable diseases
- National health programmes
- Health planning, management and economics
- Health care delivery in India

## Environment, Nutrition and Occupational Health

- Water, air and waste management
- Nutrition and nutritional programmes
- Occupational health hazards
- Maternal and child health
- Disaster management
