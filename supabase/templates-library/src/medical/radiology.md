# Foundations

## Anatomy and pathology for imaging

- Gross and cross-sectional anatomy of all body systems
- Gross morphology of systemic diseases

# System-wise Imaging and Intervention

## Imaging by system

- Chest
- Cardiovascular system
- Musculoskeletal system and soft tissue
- Gastrointestinal system
- Hepatobiliary and pancreatic system
- Genitourinary system
- Central nervous system, head and neck
- Obstetrics and gynaecology
- ENT, eye, dental and breast
- Endocrine and metabolic system
- Clinically applied radionuclide imaging

## Modalities and interventions

- Conventional radiography and mammography
- Contrast and fluoroscopic studies, including angiography
- Ultrasound, Doppler and ultrasound-guided interventions
- CT and CT-guided interventions
- MRI
- Emergency radiology

# Radiological Physics

## Radiation and equipment

- Properties of radiation and radioactivity
- Interaction of X-rays and gamma rays with matter
- X-ray generating apparatus
- Screen-film radiography and film processing
- Fluoroscopy, including flat panel units
- Computed and digital radiography
- Ultrasound, CT, MRI and DSA equipment
- Nuclear medicine equipment and isotopes

## Contrast, informatics and safety

- Iodinated, MR and ultrasound contrast media and reactions
- PACS, RIS and teleradiology
- Radiation protection, dosimetry and radiation biology
- Image quality and quality assurance
- Physics experiments: kVp, timer, mA, focal spot, filtration, protection survey

# Radiography Techniques

## Radiography and contrast techniques

- Darkroom and dry processing
- Musculoskeletal and extremity radiography
- Chest, spine, abdomen and pelvis
- Skull, orbit and sinuses
- Contrast studies of the GI and hepatobiliary tract
- Contrast studies of the CNS and cardiovascular system
- Contrast studies of the genitourinary tract
- Paediatric radiology: MCU, genitogram and bone age
- Dental, portable and emergency radiography
