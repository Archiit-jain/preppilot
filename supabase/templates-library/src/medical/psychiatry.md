# Disorders

## Organic and substance disorders

- Organic psychiatry: delirium, epilepsy, head injury, infections
- Movement disorders, including medication-induced
- Alcohol-related disorders
- Opioid, cannabis, stimulant and other substance disorders
- Nicotine and sedative-hypnotic disorders

## Psychotic and mood disorders

- Schizophrenia and related psychoses
- Delusional and brief psychotic disorders
- Depressive disorders
- Bipolar and cyclothymic disorders

## Anxiety, stress and somatic disorders

- Panic disorder, phobias and generalised anxiety
- Obsessive-compulsive disorder
- PTSD, acute stress and adjustment disorders
- Somatoform disorders
- Factitious disorders
- Dissociative disorders

## Other disorders

- Personality disorders
- Sexual disorders and gender dysphoria
- Eating disorders
- Sleep disorders
- Impulse-control disorders
- Psychosomatic disorders and consultation-liaison psychiatry
- Abuse and neglect
- Culture-bound syndromes
- Premenstrual dysphoric disorder
- Miscellaneous conditions: malingering, bereavement, non-compliance
