# N5: Some Basic Japanese

## Scripts and language knowledge

- Hiragana
- Katakana
- Basic kanji
- Basic vocabulary and grammar

## Reading

- Typical expressions and sentences in hiragana, katakana and basic kanji

## Listening

- Everyday and classroom conversations spoken slowly
- Picking out needed information from short conversations

# N4: Basic Japanese

## Language knowledge

- Basic vocabulary and kanji
- Basic grammar

## Reading

- Passages on familiar daily topics

## Listening

- Daily-life conversations spoken slowly

# N3: Everyday Japanese to a Certain Degree

## Language knowledge

- Vocabulary, kanji and grammar for everyday situations

## Reading

- Written materials on everyday topics
- Newspaper headlines
- Slightly difficult everyday writing with paraphrase help

## Listening

- Everyday conversations at near-natural speed
- Following content and relationships between speakers

# N2: Everyday and Broader Japanese

## Language knowledge

- Vocabulary, kanji and grammar for a variety of circumstances

## Reading

- Newspaper and magazine articles and commentaries
- Simple critiques
- Writings on general topics and the writer's intent

## Listening

- Conversations and news at nearly natural speed
- Following the flow and relationships of speakers

# N1: Japanese in a Variety of Circumstances

## Language knowledge

- Advanced vocabulary, kanji and grammar

## Reading

- Logically complex and abstract writing: editorials and critiques
- Writing with profound content and the writer's intent

## Listening

- Conversations, news and lectures at natural speed
- Following arguments and the speakers' relationships in detail
